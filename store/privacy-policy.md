# Netcord: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Netcord is made by TNQ Nexus.

**We don't collect any data.** Netcord has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your settings and ladder progress are stored only on your own device. They are deleted if you delete the app.

The game uses your device's vibration motor (haptics) and built-in speech voice for the chair umpire. Neither involves collecting or sending information.
